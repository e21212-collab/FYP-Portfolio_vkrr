W2

Mechanosensor Principles

Completed

Focus: Sensor physics and signal characteristics

Studied piezoelectric and PVDF-based mechanosensors. Documented frequency response curves and sensitivity ranges. Noted that sensor output is a superposition of external stimulus signal and body-motion artefact.

Key Finding: PVDF sensors show flat frequency response up to ~1 kHz — this means ego-motion artefacts cannot be removed by simple low-pass filtering alone.

Next Steps: Formally define ego-motion and collect preliminary motion data in Week 3.

W3

Ego-Motion: Definition & Characterisation

Completed

Focus: Quantifying the noise source

Defined ego-motion mathematically as the self-induced acceleration of the sensor-bearing structure. Collected baseline vibration measurements from test platform under 3 motion scenarios: slow walk, fast walk, arm swing. Plotted spectral density of each.

Key Finding: Walking gait produces dominant spectral peaks at ~2 Hz and harmonics up to ~40 Hz; arm swing extends to ~80 Hz.

Next Steps: Build experimental test rig in Week 4.
